About us

Welcome to Plastco Industries

The leading water treatment and filtration solution company — complete water treatment under one roof.

Introduction

Engineers and chemists for water and wastewater

PLASTCO designs water and wastewater treatment chemicals and equipment and builds turnkey plants with development, engineering, fabrication, testing, erection and commissioning all under one roof. The vast and varied scope of supply in essence covers the field of water and wastewater treatment technology, which also includes processes for the protection of the environment.

PLASTCO manufactures water and wastewater treatment chemicals and equipment to fit the needs of industries. PLASTCO is an organisation of engineers and chemists — teams of specialists experienced in virtually all the engineering disciplines and technologies.

Sustaining life, one drop at a time.

We are a leading water treatment plant manufacturer, iron removal plant manufacturer, membrane filtration and reverse osmosis system supplier, mineral water plant manufacturer, DM water plant manufacturer, ATM water machine manufacturer, FRP vessel manufacturer, water filter parts manufacturer, water softener manufacturer and industrial water plant manufacturer in Kolkata, West Bengal.

Plastco Industries is listed in IndiaMART’s and Trade India’s lists of verified sellers offering water treatment plants, components and related products.
