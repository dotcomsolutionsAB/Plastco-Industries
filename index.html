Plastco Industries

Quality is not a buzzword at PLASTCO

PLASTCO designs water and wastewater treatment chemicals and equipment and builds turnkey plants with development, engineering, fabrication, testing, erection and commissioning all under one roof.

The vast and varied scope of supply covers water and wastewater treatment technology, including processes for the protection of the environment. PLASTCO is an organisation of engineers and chemists — teams of specialists experienced across engineering disciplines and technologies.

An ISO 9001:2008 certified company Manufacturer, supplier and wholesaler of water treatment plants and components.

Turnkey plants

RO, iron removal, mineral water, DM and industrial water plants from design to commissioning.

Components

FRP vessels, membranes, MPV valves, housings, cartridges, pumps, UV and ozone systems.

Chemicals & service

Treatment chemicals, resins, filter media, test kits and RO purifier service support.
